How to read a chord

①Green dots are your fingers. Numbers = finger: 1 index, 2 middle, 3 ring, 4 pinky.
②○ means play the open string. × means don't play that string.
③Start slowly. Press just behind the fret, strum one string at a time, and listen for buzzing.
